# Go-To-Market Strategy

How to get your first paying customers as fast as possible.

---

## The Core Principle

**Don't sell "AI automation." Sell one specific result to one specific business.**

❌ Wrong: "We offer AI-powered automation for businesses"
✅ Right: "We help dental clinics automatically follow up with missed appointments"

---

## Step 1 — Pick One Niche First

Start with ONE niche. Master it. Then expand.

### Recommended Starting Niches (Ranked)

| Rank | Niche | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dental / Medical Clinics | Clear pain (missed appointments = lost money), easy to find, willing to pay |
| 2 | Hotels & Guesthouses | 24/7 guest questions, easy to demo, high perceived value |
| 3 | Real Estate Agents | Leads go cold fast, they know it, high commission = happy to pay |
| 4 | Restaurants | Reservation calls interrupt service, easy to understand value |
| 5 | Spas & Salons | Booking calls disrupt work, simple use case, quick to set up |

---

## Step 2 — Find 50 Prospects in 2 Hours

### Google Maps Method
1. Search `"dental clinic [your city]"` on Google Maps
2. Click each result → copy their website, phone, or email
3. Look for businesses with reviews mentioning:
   - "Hard to reach"
   - "No one answered"
   - "Waited a long time for a response"
4. These are your hottest prospects — they have the pain

### Instagram Method
1. Search `#[yourcity]clinic` or `#[yourcity]hotel`
2. Find local businesses with 200–5,000 followers
3. Check if they respond to comments — if they don't, they need your product

### Facebook Groups Method
1. Join `"[City] Business Owners"` groups
2. Search the group for posts about "customer service" or "bookings"
3. Comment helpfully, then DM

## Step 3 — Outreach Messages

### Cold Email / DM Template (Clinics)
```
Subject: Quick question about [Clinic Name]

Hi [Name],

I noticed [Clinic Name] gets a lot of appointment inquiries.

I built an AI that automatically handles booking requests and patient 
questions 24/7 — even after hours and on weekends.

One clinic I worked with reduced missed appointments by 40% in the 
first month.

I'm giving 3 clinics in [city] free access for 14 days — no credit 
card needed.

Would you be open to a 10-minute demo this week?

[Your name]
```

### Cold Email / DM Template (Hotels)
```
Subject: Quick idea for [Hotel Name]

Hi [Name],

Your front desk probably answers the same 10 guest questions every 
day — check-in time, parking, WiFi, breakfast hours.

I built an AI that handles all of these automatically, 24/7, so your 
staff can focus on in-person guests.

I'm offering 3 hotels in [city] a free 14-day trial this month.

Can I show you a quick 10-minute demo?

[Your name]
```

### WhatsApp / SMS Template (Short)
```
Hi [Name], I built an AI that handles customer bookings and questions 
24/7 for [clinics/hotels]. Free 14-day trial — no setup fee. 
Can I show you a quick demo? Takes 10 mins.
```

## Step 4 — The Demo (10 Minutes, Closes Deals)

### Structure
| Time | What to do |
|---|---|
| 0:00 – 2:00 | Show the dashboard — "This is where you manage everything" |
| 2:00 – 4:00 | Create an agent live — name it after their business |
| 4:00 – 7:00 | Type a message as a customer — show the AI reply instantly |
| 7:00 – 9:00 | Show conversation history, resolve/escalate buttons |
| 9:00 – 10:00 | Close the deal |

### Closing Line
> *"This runs 24/7 for $99/month. One extra booking per week pays for it. Want me to set it up for you today?"*

### If They Hesitate
> *"Let me activate your free 14-day trial right now. No card needed. If it doesn't work for you, just let me know and I'll cancel it. Zero risk."*

---

## Step 5 — Handle Objections

| Objection | Your Response |
|---|---|
| "It's too expensive" | "It's $99/month. One extra booking per week pays for it. The ROI is immediate." |
| "We already have staff for this" | "This works while your staff sleeps — nights, weekends, public holidays." |
| "I need to think about it" | "Totally fine. Let me set up the free trial so you can see it working in your business first." |
| "Does it really work?" | "Let me show you right now. Type a message as a customer and watch it reply." |
| "What if it says something wrong?" | "You write the rules. You control exactly what it says. And you can always escalate to a human." |
| "We're not tech-savvy" | "You don't need to be. I set it up for you. You just watch the conversations come in." |

## Pricing Strategy to Close Fast

### Standard Pricing
- Free trial: 14 days, no credit card
- After trial: $99/month (Pro plan)

### Fast-Close Offer (First 10 Customers)
> *"I'm offering the first 10 businesses a founder rate — $49/month locked in forever. After that it goes to $99."*

This creates urgency and gets you to $490 MRR fast.

### Setup Fee Option
Charge a one-time $199–$499 setup fee to configure the agent with their custom prompt, branding, and workflows. This covers your time and makes the monthly fee feel smaller.

## Outreach Channels

### Fastest Results
1. **WhatsApp / SMS** — Direct, personal, 20–30% response rate
2. **Instagram DM** — Works well for salons, restaurants, hotels
3. **Facebook Groups** — Post free trial offer, get inbound leads

### Medium-Term
4. **Cold Email** — Lower response rate but scalable
5. **LinkedIn** — Best for B2B (agencies, law firms, accountants)
6. **Google Maps reviews** — Reply to businesses with bad reviews offering help

### Passive / Long-Term
7. **Fiverr** — List "AI chatbot setup for [niche]" as a service
8. **Upwork** — Find clients looking for chatbot/automation help
9. **Content marketing** — Post before/after demos on TikTok or YouTube Shorts

## 90-Day Revenue Plan

### Month 1 — Validation
- **Goal:** 3 paying customers
- **Focus:** One niche, direct outreach, free trials
- **Target MRR:** $297

**Daily actions:**
- Send 10 outreach messages per day
- Follow up with everyone who didn't reply after 3 days
- Do at least 2 demos per week

### Month 2 — Growth
- **Goal:** 8 paying customers
- **Focus:** Referrals from Month 1 customers, expand to second niche
- **Target MRR:** $792

**Actions:**
- Ask every customer for 1 referral
- Post a case study or testimonial on social media
- Start outreach in a second niche

### Month 3 — Scale
- **Goal:** 15 customers, 3 on Pro plan
- **Focus:** Upsell existing customers, hire a VA for outreach
- **Target MRR:** $1,500–$2,000+

## Getting Referrals (Free Customers)

After a customer has been using the product for 2 weeks, send:

```
Hi [Name], glad the AI has been working well for you!

Quick favor — do you know any other [clinics/hotels/agents] in [city] 
who might benefit from this?

If you refer someone who signs up, I'll give you one month free.

No pressure at all — just thought I'd ask!
```

One happy customer in a niche knows 10 others in the same niche.
