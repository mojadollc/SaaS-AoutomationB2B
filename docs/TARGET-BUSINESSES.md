## Tier 1 — Highest Priority

These businesses have the clearest pain, the most to gain, and are easiest to close.

---

### 🏨 Hotels & Hospitality

| Detail | Info |
|---|---|
| Pain | Same guest questions 24/7, after-hours inquiries, complaint management, no-show bookings |
| Best Agents | Customer Support + Appointment Booking |
| Suggested Price | $99–$299/month |
| Decision Maker | Hotel Manager / Owner |

**Pitch:**
> "Your front desk answers the same 10 questions every day — check-in time, parking, WiFi, breakfast. Our AI handles all of them at 2am when your staff is asleep, and books rooms automatically."

**Example conversations the AI handles:**
- "What time is check-in?"
- "Do you have parking?"
- "Can I get a late checkout?"
- "I want to book a room for next Friday"
- "The AC in my room isn't working"
